# Daybook review, full-page entry and profit basis

The payment, credit/debit note, customer create/edit, subscriber create/manage, supplier, purchase return, expense and product dialogs use explicitly scoped full-page overlays. Other confirmation/security dialogs retain their existing sizes. Desktop and mobile forms fill the viewport and scroll internally.

Daybook amounts combine recorded cash and bank modes. Opening is the signed total of entries before the selected date; closing is opening plus receipts minus payments. A saved closing is a reconciliation snapshot, not a reset to zero or a transaction lock. Single-day and one-day period views show the current review state. Closing can be saved for an earlier date, including two/three days later, with a required note. Future dates are rejected using the India calendar date.

GET /api/v1/day-book uses day_book:read and no-store. POST /day-book/close requires day_book:update and {date,note}. Save and review are tenant-scoped and audited. Source entries and opening balances are recomputed in a repeatable-read snapshot; an old DayBookClosing is never used as the authoritative balance. A fingerprint of all entries through the date detects changes to the day and backdated changes affecting opening, including offsetting changes that leave closing unchanged. A changed snapshot or one predating this metadata is marked needsReview; re-save after review. Manual entries remain possible in unlocked financial years and trigger review through the same change check. Closing does not assert that the computed amount equals a verified bank/physical cash balance.

Standard P&L already selects a date range, and both P&L variants default to the active FY. Draft/cancelled sales and cancelled purchases are excluded from profit figures and matching exports. Tally Gross Profit b/f is the current selected-period Trading Account result, not prior-year profit.

The optional tenant setting ACCOUNTING/profitCarryForwardStart is a plain ISO date (e.g. 2026-04-01). Balance-sheet cumulative profit begins at that date for reports on/after it, so prior profit carry-forward is zero when the date is the selected FY start. Future years retain actual profit earned since the baseline. Earlier report dates keep their historical calculation. The basis appears in the balance-sheet screen and exports. It does not delete historical invoices, receipts, ledgers, customer openings, cash/bank balances or stock; opening-capital differences still require real reconciliation. All current local business tenants (FII, SKY and Demo Broadband) are configured to their active FY start, 2026-04-01. The reserved platform tenant is excluded. A production deployment must explicitly apply the same requested profit-only setting to all current business tenants there; source deployment alone never copies local financial data/configuration.
